Everyday Fixes

Chromebook won't turn on, won't type, or won't find Wi‑Fi

Black screen · keys do nothing · no networks listed — all three have the same first fix: a hard reset.

  1. Hold Refresh+Power together for about 2 seconds, then let go.
  2. The screen should flash and restart. Give it ten seconds.
  3. Nothing? Plug in the charger, wait a full minute with it plugged in, then repeat step 1.
  4. Still dark after two tries — stop and bring the Chromebook to your tech.

Both keys live on the top row of the Chromebook keyboard — Refresh is the circular arrow, Power sits at the far right.

Keys type the wrong characters

The keyboard language switched — look for something other than US in the corner.

  1. Press Ctrl+Space to flip back to the previous language. Test by typing a few letters.
  2. Still wrong? Click the clock at the bottom right, then open the keyboard shortcut and pick US.

The status area sits in the bottom-right corner. Clicking the time opens volume, Wi‑Fi and keyboard settings.

The screen is sideways or upside down

Display rotated 90° or 180°, usually from a stray shortcut.

Press Ctrl+Shift+Refresh and confirm when the box appears. Each press turns the display another quarter turn, so keep pressing until it's upright.

Everything looks huge or tiny? That's the resolution, not rotation. Press Ctrl+Shift+0 (zero) to put the screen back to its default. If only one website looks wrong, that's page zoom instead — Ctrl+0 resets it.

No sound

Work through these in order — the first one is the answer about nine times out of ten.

  1. Check the volume. Click the time at the bottom right, then drag the volume slider up. If the speaker icon has a line through it, click it to unmute.
  2. Check where the sound is going. Next to the slider, click the arrow › and choose the right output. Use the same panel to pick your microphone input.
  3. Unplug anything you've connected. Headphones and speakers keep the sound to themselves — pull them out and test the built-in speakers.
  4. Check the site itself. Many videos and testing tools have their own mute button and their own volume slider.

Browser & Websites

A site, tool or extension is acting strange

Pages won't load, buttons don't respond, or you keep seeing an old version of a page.

  1. Press Ctrl+H to open your history — or click the ⋮ menu at the top right and choose History → History.
  2. Click Clear browsing data in the left menu.
  3. Set the time range to All time.
  4. Check Cookies and other site data and Cached images and files, then click Clear data.
  5. Close the tab and open the site again.

Heads up: this signs you out of most websites. You'll log back in through ClassLink as usual — no work is deleted.

LockDown Browser won't open

It crashes on launch or closes straight away. Resetting Chrome clears the settings blocking it.

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Click the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Settings.
  3. Choose Reset settings in the left menu.
  4. Click Restore settings to their original defaults.
  5. Uncheck the reporting box, then click Reset settings.
  6. Reopen LockDown Browser.

Your bookmarks, history and saved passwords stay. Pinned tabs and extension settings are cleared.

Testing Days

TestNav won't close once you're finished

The test is submitted but TestNav stays on screen and the normal exit does nothing.

  1. Submit the test first. Make sure you've reached the confirmation screen — this shortcut closes the app, it doesn't save anything.
  2. Hold Alt+R+Vol Up together to force TestNav closed.
  3. Only ever do this once you've finished. Never force-quit, restart or shut down in the middle of a test — raise your hand instead. Closing TestNav before you submit can lock your session.

The screen jumps or scrolls during testing

The page flickers, scrolls on its own, or keeps pulling focus away from the question.

This one isn't a student fix. The Securly monitoring extension is fighting with the testing software.

  1. Tell your teacher what the screen is doing.
  2. Teacher: turn Securly off for the class, let the student finish, then turn it back on as soon as testing ends.

Hardware

Camera or microphone doesn't work

Black camera window, no green light, or nobody can hear you on a call.

  1. Open the site that needs the camera — permissions are set per site.
  2. In the address bar, click the icon left of the web address — two short lines with a circle on each:
  3. Choose Site settings. Find Camera and Microphone in the permissions list, set both to Allow, then reload the page.
  4. Close every other tab or app that might be holding the camera, then reload the page.
  5. Do a hard reset: Refresh+Power for 2 seconds.
  6. Still nothing? It's a hardware issue — see your tech. Don't keep trying settings.
